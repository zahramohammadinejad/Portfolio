CASE STUDY 03 · SYSTEM ANALYSIS

University Research
Portal

Requirements Analysis & SharePoint Implementation Support

A university research portal project focused on translating user and organizational needs into documented requirements, forms, workflows, and a practical SharePoint-based solution.

Role System AnalystPlatform Microsoft SharePointFocus Requirements & Solution Analysis
01

PROJECT CONTEXT

From organizational needs to a working portal

The Research Deputy of a university required an organizational portal to support its users and operational activities. The project involved understanding how users worked, identifying their requirements, documenting the expected functionality, and translating those requirements into SharePoint forms and workflows.

My contribution covered the analysis and coordination layer between stakeholders and the implementation team, with involvement through solution review and final client delivery.

⌘

Project Objective

Translate user needs into clear system requirements and support the implementation of a usable, structured SharePoint portal.

SOLUTIONForm + workflow in SharePoint

Turning needs into actionable system requirements

Rather than treating requirements as isolated statements, I worked through the connection between the user's objective, the required system behavior, and the practical solution to be implemented.

  • Clarified user needs through interviews and discussions
  • Documented functional requirements for the implementation team
  • Identified required forms and workflow behavior
  • Supported alignment between requirements and the implemented solution
