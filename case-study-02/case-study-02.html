01 · PROJECT CONTEXT

Moving from process design into implementation

Following the process analysis and improvement phase, the redesigned processes moved into development and implementation. At this stage, my involvement expanded from process analysis toward requirements coordination, prioritization, implementation follow-up and user feedback management.

The goal was to keep the developing solution aligned with approved business requirements while responding to practical needs discovered during implementation.

03 · TEAM & STAKEHOLDERS

Working across business, IT and development

The project team consisted of three people on our side. I worked as the Business & Process Analyst, alongside the Project Manager and an intern. On the client side, our work involved process owners, the IT manager, development team members and different groups of users.

The collaboration required continuous communication between business needs and implementation constraints.

05 · REQUIREMENT PRIORITIZATION

Not every request became a development task immediately.

When a new request was raised, I helped assess its business relevance, process impact, dependencies and urgency before it was considered for implementation.
