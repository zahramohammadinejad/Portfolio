PROJECT CONTEXT

Understanding the process before redesigning it.

The project was part of a broader process identification and improvement initiative for a Construction Engineering Organization in the building and construction domain. Our company worked as an external contractor to identify and improve business processes and prepare structured documentation for implementation.

This case study focuses on Construction File Formation as one representative process from the larger initiative.

MY ROLE

CHALLENGE ANALYSIS

What we looked for during process analysis.

The analysis focused on missing or repeated information, handoffs that could create delays, unclear responsibilities, and opportunities to make the workflow more structured and implementable.
